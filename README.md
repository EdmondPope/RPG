A game made in Godot where you play as a lone survivor running from an immortal snail across a massive handcrafted tileset world. The game is built around a long continuous path that stretches across multiple environments and locations, creating an endless feeling of travel and survival while the snail slowly follows behind the player at all times.

The project features smooth top-down movement, Y-sorting for realistic depth between objects and characters, and collision systems that allow the environment to feel detailed and interactive. Trees, buildings, fences, rocks, abandoned vehicles, signs, and other obstacles properly block movement and create narrow spaces that the player must carefully navigate through while escaping the immortal snail.

The world is created using layered tilesets that bring the environment to life with roads, forests, abandoned towns, tunnels, campsites, rivers, and ruined structures spread across the path. Different sections of the map have their own atmosphere and visual style, making the journey feel larger and more immersive as the player continues moving forward.

The immortal snail acts as a constant source of pressure throughout the game. It slowly moves toward the player no matter where they go and can never truly be defeated, removed, or escaped forever. Even though the snail moves slowly, its unstoppable nature forces the player to keep traveling, avoid getting trapped by obstacles, and continue progressing through the world. The closer the snail gets, the more intense the gameplay becomes.

The game focuses heavily on atmosphere and tension. Long empty roads, dark forests, isolated buildings, and quiet environments create a feeling of loneliness while the player constantly knows the snail is somewhere behind them. The combination of exploration and constant pursuit creates a unique survival experience where movement and decision making are extremely important.

The project also demonstrates several important game development systems inside Godot including:

TileMap world building
Y-sorting systems
Collision handling
CharacterBody2D movement
Enemy AI behavior
Path following
Scene organization
Sprite animation
Camera systems
Long-form level design

As the player continues down the endless path, the environments become more dangerous and complex, forcing faster reactions and smarter movement. Tight corridors, obstacle-filled areas, and difficult terrain increase the challenge while the immortal snail continues its slow but inevitable chase.

The game is designed as a suspenseful survival experience that combines simple mechanics with constant psychological pressure. The idea of an enemy that never stops moving creates tension at every moment, turning even small obstacles or wrong turns into dangerous situations. Every section of the path feels important because stopping for too long could allow the immortal snail to finally catch up.

Overall, the project is a large-scale top-down survival chase game built in Godot that combines tileset world design, Y-sorting, collisions, smooth movement, and enemy pursuit mechanics into a tense and atmospheric experience centered around escaping an unstoppable immortal snail across one massive continuous path.
